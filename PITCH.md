## 1. Digital Vulnerability & High-Value Opportunity Audit

1. **Broken Directory Link Redirecting Customers to Spam (`borrellis.co.uk` → `survey-keys.com`):**
   - Third-party food directories (including Restaurant Guru) still list `borrellis.co.uk` as their website — which currently **302-redirects prospective customers straight to `http://survey-keys.com` domain-parking spam**.
2. **Clean Replacement Domain Available Right Now (`borrellideli.co.uk`):**
   - `borrellideli.co.uk` is confirmed **unregistered via Nominet (checked 12 Nov 2026)** and available to register in the owner's name for ~£10/year. Updating Google Business Profile and local directories to `borrellideli.co.uk` immediately cuts off the spam redirect.
3. **Conflicting Directory Opening Hours:**
   - Public directory listings contradict each other on daytime closing times and Friday/Saturday late hours — risking wasted trips or missed Friday bookings. An official site gives one authoritative source of truth.
4. **Massive Local Goodwill (~171 Google Reviews at 4.8★) With Zero Web Home:**
   - ~171 Google reviews averaging **4.8★** prove huge local loyalty, yet tucked down cobbled Danby Wynd off High Street footfall, first-time visitors searching for **Friday "Piatto Di Antipasto Night"** or seasonal Italian gift hampers have nowhere to view tiers or pre-order online.

## Set D — Build Your Board (`/d/`)

- **Live Concept URL:** https://tedotedo.github.io/borrelli-demo/d/
- **What it is:** Italian tin-label styling and a board builder: tap salumi, cheese and antipasti onto a wooden board, then send it to the counter.
- **Reach it from any page:** tap **D** in the Concept switcher in the top banner.

### 3-Tap Phone Demo (Set D)
1. Tap **Start your board**, then tap five or six items: each drops onto the board and the tally changes from "A board for two" to "A board for four".
2. Pick a collection date, add a name and tap **Send to the counter**.
3. Scroll to the **Piatto Di Antipasto Night** ticket showing the next Friday.

### One-Line Pitch
> *"Customers build the board on their phone, and you get a tidy list instead of a phone call at lunchtime."*

### Know Before You Show
- The twelve counter items are generic categories for illustration, not the confirmed range, and no prices are shown. The Google rating is left as a placeholder until re-checked.
- Demo only: no form sends anything, and unknown details stay as bracketed `[...]` placeholders for the owner to confirm.
